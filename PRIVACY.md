# Tawazun Privacy Policy (Plain Language)

*Last updated: November 2026 • Karachi, Pakistan*

At **Tawazun**, our primary mission is to protect Pakistani children while enabling their educational growth. We understand that Pakistani students require their smartphones to access homework, class notes, and teacher announcements on WhatsApp. Our privacy policy is straightforward, transparent, and legally binding.

---

## 1. What We Collect

1. **Parent Account Information**: Parent email address, password hash, and subscription verification token from Google Play.
2. **Child Profile**: Nickname (e.g., "Zain"), grade level, device model, and avatar emoji. Children do not have personal accounts or email credentials.
3. **App Usage Timestamps**: App package names and active usage duration in minutes.
4. **Web Domain Attempts**: Visited web domains (hostnames only) evaluated by our safety filter to block harmful or distracting content.
5. **Screen Mirror Frames**: Captured frames during active parent-initiated safety checks.

---

## 2. What We NEVER Collect

- **NO Phone Numbers / SMS**: We do not collect phone numbers, send SMS OTPs, or read private SMS messages.
- **NO Credit Card Data**: Subscriptions are processed directly by Google Play Billing (PKR 299/mo). No payment cards are ever stored.
- **NO Private WhatsApp Message Contents**: We do NOT scrape or sell personal chat messages. In read-only mode, only the local message composition input is disabled when allowance runs out; incoming educational documents remain strictly private to the student.
- **NO Location Tracking / GPS**: Tawazun does not track physical child coordinates.

---

## 3. Non-Negotiable 3-Day Deletion Guarantee

Children deserve privacy. Surveillance data must not exist indefinitely:

- **Screen mirror frames**: Deleted permanently after **3 days**.
- **Individual app usage events**: Deleted permanently after **3 days**.
- **Web navigation attempts**: Deleted permanently after **3 days**.
- **Instant Purge Button**: Parents can press "Delete now" at any time from the dashboard to instantly delete all 3-day monitoring data for their child.
- **Aggregated usage**: Only numerical daily minute totals (e.g., "WhatsApp: 45 min on Nov 8") are kept up to 30 days for weekly parental reports.
